The Concept

A cafe interior designed around the pace of coffee — the quick order at the counter, the long conversation at a corner table. Warm materials and tactile finishes make every surface feel considered.

Seating is arranged to create a gentle rhythm between counter and table, so the space stays alive whether it is a quiet morning or a busy afternoon.
