The Concept

A rental PS store that turns gaming into a hangout. Bold colour blocks and high-energy displays make the store feel electric the moment you step in.

Flexible seating and clear circulation make it easy to wait, watch, and play — designed around the flow of customers and consoles so everyone finds their spot.

The Gaming Zone

Bold colour and smart sightlines turn every corner of the store into a place where the action is always visible and everyone feels part of the game.

Energy in the Details

Vibrant accents and graphic touches give the store a confident, playful identity that matches the energy of the games on screen.

The Full Scene

From the entrance to the play stations, the layout keeps everything within easy reach and view — a store built for hanging around, not just picking up and leaving.
