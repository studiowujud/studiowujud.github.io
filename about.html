About Us

The story behind Studio Wujud and how we approach every space.

Our Story

Studio Wujud was born from a simple belief: that great design has the power to transform how we feel, connect, and live. Founded in 2020, we set out to create interiors that prioritize warmth, authenticity, and thoughtful detail over trends.

Our name — wujud, meaning "form" or "presence" in Indonesian — reflects our commitment to giving shape to your vision. Every project is a collaboration, guided by your story and brought to life through meticulous craftsmanship.

Based in Jakarta, we work on residential and commercial projects across Indonesia, partnering with trusted artisans and makers to realize spaces that are truly one of a kind.

01

Listen First

We begin every project by understanding your needs, habits, and aspirations. Design decisions follow from real understanding, not assumptions.

02

Honor the Context

Every space has unique qualities — light, proportion, history. We work with what exists rather than against it, enhancing rather than overpowering.

03

Less but Better

We believe in quality over quantity. Carefully chosen pieces, natural materials, and thoughtful restraint create spaces that age gracefully.
